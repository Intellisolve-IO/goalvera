Goalvera website privacy notice

Effective November 12, 2026. This notice covers the website at goalvera.app only. It does not cover the Goalvera app or classroom recorder, which a school agreement covers.

Who we are. goalvera.app is run by Intellisolve, LLC, 1460 Bates Ct NE, Brookhaven, GA 30319, USA. Contact: privacy@intellisolve.io.

What we collect. The site has no forms and no sign-in. We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages and buttons (such as "Try Now" and our email link) are used. Google Analytics sets cookies and receives your browser and device type, the pages you view, the site that sent you, and an approximate location (city or region) worked out from your IP address. We never send names, email addresses or anything you type. Google signals and advertising features are off.

Email. If you email sales@intellisolve.io, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use it only to reply.

Sharing. Google processes analytics data for us as our service provider. We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't show ads.

Retention. Google Analytics keeps event data for 14 months, then deletes it. We keep emails for as long as needed to respond and follow up.

Your choices. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's opt-out browser add-on. The site works without them.

Children. The site is for teachers, school staff and other adults. It isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes. If we change this notice, we'll update the date above.
