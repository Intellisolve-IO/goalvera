Feature Overview • Transcript-Level Phonetic AI

AI That Listens Like a Specialist—Down to the Exact Phoneme.

Standard voice-to-text tools are built for boardrooms—they auto-correct or gloss over the exact moments a special needs child learning English struggles.

Goalvera’s AI does the opposite. Built specifically for Special Education and language acquisition, it analyzes classroom audio at the raw transcript and phonetic level so every articulation hurdle, pronunciation pattern, and behavioral cue is captured as actionable evidence.

Pinpoint Phonetic Breakdown

Detects phoneme substitutions, vowel distortions, stuttering blocks, and language transfer patterns without erasing them.

Evidence-Linked Forms & Evaluations

Pre-fills evaluations, worksheets, and incident forms with exact classroom transcript timestamps—no more reconstructing a breakthrough at 4:00 PM.

A Full Week of Classroom Sessions, Distilled Into a Warm 1-Minute Parent Note.

Goalvera analyzes speech at the raw phoneme and transcript level—capturing consonant blend substitutions, language transfer patterns, and hesitation pauses—and links those exact timestamps directly to progress forms.
