Your students' goals, a little closer every day.

Goalvera is a teaching assistant for special education teachers. It reads each student's education plan and your classroom sessions, then gives you daily coaching ideas, drafts your weekly parent notes, and fills in the first draft of your paperwork. You review everything. You decide what goes out.

How it works

  1. Add each student's education plan. Goalvera uses the goals in the plan as the starting point for everything it suggests.
  2. Record your sessions. A small classroom recorder captures the session so Goalvera has a transcript to work from. (An iPhone demo is coming soon.)
  3. Get your daily email. At the end of each day you get coaching suggestions tied to each student's goals, plus tools to start tomorrow's lesson plan.
  4. Review the weekly drafts. Parent summaries and forms show up as drafts. You read them, change what you want, and send or file them yourself.

The daily email

At the end of every day, you get one email that is written for you and your students.

  • What happened today: a short read on each student's day, tied back to the goals in their plan.
  • Coaching suggestions: practical ideas for tomorrow, based on the student's plan and what actually happened in the session.
  • Lesson plan tools: a starting point for tomorrow's lesson plan that you can adjust in a few minutes.

It is a suggestion, not a script. You know your students; Goalvera just helps you see the day clearly.

The weekly parent summary

At the end of the week, Goalvera drafts a summary for each student's family. You review it, edit it, and send it yourself. Nothing goes to a parent without you.

  • Written in plain language a family can read in a minute.
  • Tied to the goals in the student's plan.
  • Your words have the final say: change the tone, add a note, or leave something out.

Forms, drafted for your review

Evaluations, worksheets and incident forms come pre-filled with suggestions from your classroom transcripts, so you're not rebuilding the day from memory.

  • Each suggestion shows what was said in class, so you can check it at a glance.
  • You check it, correct it and approve it before it is used anywhere.
  • You spend your time on judgment, not retyping.

The recorder and the demo

The classroom recorder (DIY kit). Goalvera runs on one device: the Hosyond ESP32-S3 3.5" board, in a 3D-printed case. It is low-cost and built from a parts list. We help you set it up.

  • Records classroom sessions and uploads them over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
  • Audio recordings are deleted from cloud storage after 7 days.
  • Open source, so you can run it yourself if your district prefers.

Try the demo.

  • With the DIY kit: build the recorder and try it in a demo account.
  • With an iPhone (coming soon): record from Safari on your iPhone, no app to install. The iPhone recorder is for the demo only; the Hosyond board is the supported classroom device.

The demo uses made-up sample data and a sample lesson script. Please don't record real students in the demo.

FAQ

Who is Goalvera for?

Special education teachers who want more time with students and less time on paperwork.

Does Goalvera send anything to parents on its own?

No. Weekly summaries are drafts. You review them, edit them and send them yourself.

Are the forms filled in for me?

They are drafted for you. Evaluations, worksheets and incident forms are suggested from your classroom transcripts, and you review and approve every one before it's used.

What do I need to record sessions?

The Hosyond ESP32-S3 3.5" board, which is the only supported classroom device. An iPhone demo is coming soon.

What happens to recordings and student information?

Goalvera is open source, so you can read the code and run it yourself if your district prefers. Audio is uploaded over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Audio recordings are deleted from cloud storage after 7 days.

What does it cost?

The code is free under Apache 2.0. Email us about setup and support for your school.
